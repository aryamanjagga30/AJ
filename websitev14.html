About

I grew up in Chandigarh, Le Corbusier's city of dreams carved from concrete yet very close to nature with a modernist vision. At 14, I was already drawing at a professional level, refining skills that would shape everything to come. A Bachelor's in Information Arts and Design Practices opened doors to everything that fascinated me: sound design, filmmaking, graphic design, performance art, and experimental music.

Unpaid freelance grind taught me hustle. Then McCann Worldgroup happened rising from an intern to Junior Art Director, working late nights crafting campaigns for giants like Microsoft, IKEA, Airtel, Hero, Air India & Lufthansa and dozens more. From aviation to tech, telecom to education, I learned how global brands speak to millions.

UI/UX Design Adobe Creative Suite Illustration Web Design

Hungry for more, I'm now at Dublin City University pursuing the M.Sc. Emerging Media programme, highly competitive, brutally specialized. Game prototypes, immersive soundscapes, apps that blur reality with fiction. I'm building worlds, not just designing screens.

Sound became more than a side passion. In 2025, I became a verified artist, my tracks streaming on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, SoundCloud, everywhere. Currently, I'm building the social media platform humanity actually deserves. Design, tech, and real human connection, no bullshit, with so much more to come in my years ahead.
